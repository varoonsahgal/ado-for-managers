The portfolio you'll steer

Project Lighthouse: HarborPoint's transformation

HarborPoint is a member-owned financial cooperative modernizing how 1.9 million members bank, get help and stay safe. The Executive Committee has ranked four strategic priorities. Every Feature you inspect serves one of them, or it should.

1
Fraud Detection & Security

Protect every member: real-time fraud alerts, adaptive MFA, takeover protection.

2
Digital Banking Modernization

Digital that feels human: mobile deposit, card controls, online account opening.

3
Customer Support Modernization

Effortless support: omnichannel service requests, secure messaging and chat.

4
Branch Experience Transformation

Branches as relationship hubs: appointments, lobby check-in, in-branch onboarding.

E
Platform Foundations

The enablers underneath: notification service and member API gateway.

!
Compliance: Examination Readiness

Mandatory, with a fixed date. Watch where it sits in the backlog, and where it sits in reality.
