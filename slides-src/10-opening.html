Welcome everyone. Set the frame: this is not a click-training course. Everyone here already uses Azure DevOps. For three days we use it the way a manager should: to answer the questions leadership actually asks, and to make decisions with evidence.

Every manager's Monday

Are we going to make it? What do you need from me?

Dana Whitfield · EVP, Member Experience

Who's been asked this in the last month? How long did your answer take?

Azure DevOps holds the answer. This course is about getting it out fast, honestly, and at the right altitude.

Ask the room: how many of you got a version of this question last week? How long did it take to answer? Where did the numbers come from? That gap between the question and a trustworthy answer is what we close.

Our altitude
