# Training

This section belongs to participants in the **Azure DevOps for Managers** course. Each participant has a participant ID, `P01` to `P30`, given by the instructor. On this page, `P##` means your own ID.

## Your wiki pages

Create all of your pages under **`/Training/P##/`**. For example:

- `/Training/P07/Sprint Delivery Report`
- `/Training/P07/Executive Briefing`

To start, create a page named `P##` under this Training page, then add your pages beneath it. Copy templates from [Templates](/Templates) instead of editing them.

## Your conventions in Azure DevOps

| Thing | Convention |
|---|---|
| Sandbox area | `HarborPoint\Training\P##` |
| Sandbox team | **P## Sandbox** (you are its team administrator) |
| Queries | `Shared Queries/Training/P##/` (or *My Queries* for scratch work) |
| Dashboards | Project dashboard named `P## - <name>` |
| Delivery Plans | `P## - <name>` |
| Wiki | `/Training/P##/<page>` |
| Work items you create | Only in your sandbox area. Prefix Feature titles with `P## - ` |

## What is already in your sandbox

- A Feature **P## - Omnichannel Service Requests (sandbox)** with a small copy of a team's backlog, for data clean-up practice.
- A Feature **P## - Instant Card Controls (planning copy)** with the same stories as the real Feature, for forecasting practice.
- A query folder `Shared Queries/Training/P##/Lab 11 - Inherited queries` with copies of existing reports pointed at your sandbox.

You may change anything in your own sandbox freely.

## Ground rules

- The six delivery teams' work items, boards, team settings and dashboards are **read-only** for you. Do not edit, move, re-order or re-estimate their items.
- Do not edit pages outside `/Training/P##/`.
- Name everything you create with your `P##` prefix so the instructor can find it and clean up afterwards.
