# Project Lighthouse: Member Experience Transformation Program

> *Steady hands. Safe harbor.*

Welcome to the home of the **Member Experience Transformation Program (METP)**, known across HarborPoint Financial as **Project Lighthouse**. This wiki is where the program keeps its charter, objectives, team information, research and working practices.

HarborPoint Financial is a member-owned financial cooperative founded in 1952 by dockworkers and harbor pilots in Port Haven. Today we serve **1.9 million members** through **74 branches**, with about **3,100 employees** and **$38B in assets**. Project Lighthouse modernizes how members bank, get help and stay safe.
