## Headline findings

These are the findings the Executive Committee has asked the program to respond to.

| Finding | Figure |
|---|---|
| Demand for **branch appointments** among members **55+** | **+28%** compared with the previous quarter |
| Members naming **fraud and scams** as their top financial worry | **71%**. Fraud is the **#1 worry in every member segment** |
| **Digital-only** members' rating of the **onboarding** experience | **2.9 / 5** |

### Branch appointments are in demand

Members aged 55 and over booked and requested branch appointments far more often than last quarter (+28%). They want time with a person for account changes, fraud concerns and help with digital tools.

### Fraud is everyone's top worry

71% of members named fraud or scams as their top financial worry. It ranked first in every segment we report on: by age band, by primary channel (branch-first, mixed, digital-only) and by tenure. Members asked for faster alerts and an easy way to say "that wasn't me".

### Digital-only members rate onboarding poorly

Digital-only members gave onboarding 2.9 out of 5. The most common complaints were ID verification failures, having to start over, and waiting for approval. This is consistent with the [Onboarding Research Findings](/Research/Onboarding-Research-Findings).

## Leadership response

- **Hector Alvarez** (VP, Branch Network) has asked the program to **move one Digital Accounts engineer to F18 In-Branch Digital Onboarding**, citing the rise in branch-appointment demand among members 55+.
- **Simone Laurent** has asked that fraud protection remain the program's first priority.
- Both requests are on the agenda for PI 5 Planning.

## Method notes

- Online and paper survey sent to a random sample of members, stratified by age band and primary channel. Paper surveys were available in branches for members who do not use digital channels.
- Results are weighted to the member base by age band and channel.
- Quarter-on-quarter changes are reported only where the question wording did not change.
