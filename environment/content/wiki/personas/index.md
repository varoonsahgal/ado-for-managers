# Personas

Starter personas for member onboarding and service design, built from the [Onboarding Research Findings](/Research/Onboarding-Research-Findings) and the [Member Pulse Survey Q3](/Research/Member-Pulse-Survey-Q3).

These are **starter** personas. Each one has sections marked *To be developed* for product teams to complete from research. Use the [Persona Canvas](/Templates/Persona-Canvas) template when creating a new persona.

| Persona | In one line | Work item tag |
|---|---|---|
| [Dee Harmon](/Personas/Dee-Harmon) | 68, 41-year member, branch-first, wary of fraud, wants a person she knows | `persona-dee` |
| [Jordan Reyes](/Personas/Jordan-Reyes) | 24, mobile-only, applies late at night, expects instant approval | `persona-jordan` |
| [Amara Okafor](/Personas/Amara-Okafor) | 37, military spouse who relocates often, starts online and finishes in branch | `persona-amara` |

Tag user stories with the persona tag so we can see which members each Feature serves.
