## Snapshot

Jordan just started an apprenticeship at the harbor and needs an account for direct deposit. Jordan does everything on a phone and has never been inside a bank branch. Most of Jordan's admin happens late in the evening, after a long shift. Jordan's application to HarborPoint was started at 11 pm.

## Goals

- Open an account in one sitting, on a phone.
- Get approved instantly and start using a card right away.
- Set up direct deposit before the first paycheck.

## Frustrations

- Steps that fail without explaining why.
- Being told to visit a branch or call during business hours.
- Waiting days for approval.

## Behavior

Jordan abandons at friction. If a step fails twice, Jordan closes the app and tries a competitor. Jordan does not read long instructions and expects the app to guide each step.

## Channels and devices

- Mid-range Android phone, often on mobile data.
- Uses chat, never phone calls.
- Has never visited a branch.
