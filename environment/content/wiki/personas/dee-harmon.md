## Snapshot

Dee kept the books at the Port Haven shipyard for most of her working life. She is precise with money, reads every statement and balances her account by hand. She visits her local branch most weeks and knows several staff by name. Her daughter set up online banking for her once, but Dee has not logged in since.

## Goals

- Keep her savings safe.
- Get answers from a person she trusts, not a menu.
- Understand exactly what she is agreeing to before she signs.

## Frustrations

- Being told to "just do it in the app".
- Long waits in the branch when the person she knows is busy.
- Paperwork she has already filled in being asked for again.

## Fraud and trust

Dee is wary of fraud. A friend from church lost money to a text message scam, and Dee now distrusts any message that asks her to click a link. She would rather call the branch than respond to an alert.

## Channels and devices

- Visits the branch most weeks.
- Owns a smartphone, mainly for calls and photos of her grandchildren.
- Has online banking credentials but does not use them.

## Relevant research

- 62% of branch-onboarded members over 60 never activate digital banking.
- Demand for branch appointments among members 55+ is up 28%.
- Fraud is the #1 worry in every member segment.
