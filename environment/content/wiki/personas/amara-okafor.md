## Snapshot

Amara's family relocates every two to three years with military postings. Each move means new accounts, new addresses and new paperwork. She runs a small online business selling handmade goods, so she juggles personal and business banking. She starts applications online between other tasks, often over several sessions, and usually ends up in a branch to finish.

## Goals

- Start an application, stop, and pick it up later without losing her work.
- Upload documents from wherever she is, instead of carrying papers to a branch.
- Keep banking with the same institution through every move.

## Frustrations

- Applications that time out and have to be restarted.
- Branch staff re-entering information she already gave online.
- Address and residency checks that assume people stay in one place.

## Needs

- **Save and resume** across sessions and devices.
- **Remote document upload** for ID, proof of address and military orders.
- A branch visit that picks up where her online application left off.

## Channels and devices

- Laptop for business, phone for everything else.
- Visits a branch when a step cannot be finished online.

## Relevant research

- Members who start online and then visit a branch re-enter their data 83% of the time.
- Branch onboarding averages 47 minutes.
